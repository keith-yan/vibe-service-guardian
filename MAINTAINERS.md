## Maintainer responsibilities

- enforce the local-only privacy and explicit-confirmation boundaries;
- review security-sensitive process, network, log, snapshot, and model-runtime changes;
- keep tests, dependency locks, SBOM generation, platform claims, and validation records accurate;
- distinguish code adaptation, automated fixture validation, and real target-platform acceptance;
- triage issues and pull requests without asking users to publish secrets or raw local databases;
- publish releases, checksums, and known limitations only after the release checklist passes.

## Becoming a maintainer

A contributor may be invited as a maintainer only after sustained, reviewable contributions and demonstrated understanding of the security and privacy model. The primary maintainer records the scope—triage, documentation, platform ownership, security, or release—and grants the least repository permission needed.
